Why Wonderland

Escrow is a detail business. We are detail people.

Escrow is a neutral third party holding funds and documents until every condition of the purchase agreement has been met. That sounds simple. In practice it is hundreds of small deadlines, and one missed signature can move a closing by a week.

Wonderland exists because the best escrow officers in Southern California wanted somewhere to do this properly — with the systems, security and support that let them stay ahead of every file instead of reacting to it.

Wire Fraud Protection

Never take wire instructions from an email

Real estate wire fraud is the single most expensive thing that can happen in a transaction, and it almost always starts with a convincing email. Confirm wiring instructions by phone, using a number you already had.

How We Protect You
  • Verbal verification of every wire, every time
  • We never change wire instructions mid-transaction
  • Encrypted document delivery, not email attachments
  • Same-business-day confirmation that funds landed
