Ana Sanchez

Senior Escrow Officer | Branch Manager

Ana started in title in 2001 as a Title Assistant and was quickly promoted to Escrow Officer. Working through 2008 she handled 100–200 refinances and 50–60 sales a month in a foreclosure market.
